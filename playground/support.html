Support SonicFrame

SonicFrame is free, MIT-licensed, and it stays that way. One person builds it. If it saves you time, your support pays for the next releases, the tests that keep them safe to update, the docs with live examples, and the accessibility work that lets people who can't see the screen play, seek and follow a sound too.
