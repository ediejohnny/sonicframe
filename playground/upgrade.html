Upgrade guide

From 0.4 to 0.5. Every new feature is off until you turn it on, so an upgrade changes almost nothing on its own; the things that do are listed first.

Update the package

npm install sonicframe@0.5

What can look different

The focus ring of a bordered control

A control with a border of its own (the player's speed button, an outlined play button) now shows its keyboard focus ring on that border, instead of 2 px around it: one line, not a border and a ring side by side. Controls without a border keep the ring 2 px out.

The parts beside the wave line up with the bars

The play button and the time beside a wave sat lower than the bars when a plugin added a strip above or below them. They now line up with the bars' middle.

The time shows the second a seek asked for

A browser can land a seek a hair short (an MP3 asked for 0:15 can start at 14.998). The player's time and the slider's words now show 0:15 there, not 0:14; with preload="none", they show where a seek before play will start, not 0:00.

What you can turn on

New Where to read about it
Chapters from WebVTT or Podcasting 2.0 JSON (chapters(), readChapters, loadChapters) Chapters
The player's chapters option, its list (popover or drawer, listLook) and the tag's chapters attribute Chapters on the player
underStart, metaWrap, long names (nameOverflow, nameTooltip, marquee) Parts and layouts
frame.time and formatPosition Frame methods and events

For plugin authors

Nothing changes for a plugin written for 0.4. To read where the sound is, prefer frame.time to the media's currentTime: before the media loads, it already says where a seek will start it.

Fixed

  • A step to a whole second no longer shows one second less (0:04 for 0:05).
  • Centered on short bars, the play button no longer sticks out of the player's line.
  • These docs: the fade between pages, and a site a screen reader and a keyboard can move through.

Every change is in the changelog.
