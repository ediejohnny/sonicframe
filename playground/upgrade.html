Upgrade guide

What changes from one version to the next, the newest first: from 1.2 to 1.3, then back to 0.6. Nothing breaks: every new feature is off until you turn it on (the one exception, a sound that cannot load now says so, is listed below), and every old name keeps working until 2.0.

Update the package

npm install sonicframe@1

In 1.0: nothing to change

1.0 is 0.8 with a promise: from here on SonicFrame follows Semantic Versioning, so a 1.x version never breaks code written for 1.0, and old names keep working until 2.0. Versions and support says what the promise covers.

  • Every version is published from the repository's GitHub workflow, with npm provenance.
  • A vulnerability is reported privately on GitHub, as SECURITY.md explains.
  • The package names where to support the project: npm fund shows it.

In 0.8: what works differently

Regions tell onChange when a drag ends

onChange of regions() is called once when a drag is let go, and after each change by the API or the keys; it was also called at every move of a drag. An app that saves the regions there now saves once. To follow the times while they move, use the new onInput, called at every move of a drag that changes a region. 

regions({
    onInput: (list) => showTimes(list), // at every move of a drag
    onChange: (list) => save(list), // once it is let go
});

Regions tell only real changes

No onChange comes for a press on a region that moves nothing, for remove() of an id no region has or for an update() that changes nothing, and no onSelect when the selection stays the same. A region still being drawn when the drag is cut short goes away with only an onInput, since onChange never heard of it.

The tag keeps its sound

<sonic-player> builds its player again for an attribute it cannot change live, and keeps the same audio: the sound goes on where it was, at its volume and speed. A tag moved to another place on the page keeps its player. Only a new src or preload brings a new sound, so a page that counted on a rebuild to stop the sound now pauses it itself.

Some keys go on to the page

R with no region, and M with no marker, let the key go on to the page, where the player's own keys or yours can answer it. The keys pressed on the minimap's slider stay its own: Delete, Enter, C, M and the zoom keys no longer reach the wave behind it.

In 0.8: fixed

  • Regions keep an id of their own: a file read back with region-1 no longer makes two regions share it, which removed both.
  • A region whose start or end is not a number is refused, and a looping region that is removed stops playing.
  • The volume bar takes the focus when it is pressed, so its keys work right after a click.
  • formatTime with decimals shows 1:00.0, never 0:60.0.
  • Player.destroy() stops the download of the sound it made.

The other fixes of 0.8 are in the changelog.

In 0.7: what can look or work differently

Chapter lines follow the palette

Every palette now sets --sonicframe-chapter, so the chapters' lines no longer stay gray on the dark and contrast palettes.

Two names are kept for the recorder's own icons

The recorder screen no longer shows an app button named resume or cancel. On <sonic-recorder>, its icon-resume or icon-cancel would draw both that button and the screen's own. Give such a button another name.

Silences keep their numbers in range

findSilences keeps its windowSeconds from 0.001 to 1 second, and silencesFromPeaks its threshold from 0.001 to 0.45, as outlineSound already did. A threshold over 0.5 used to leave a silence open to the end of the sound.

In 0.7: the new names

0.7 gave one idea one name across the library. Each old name works the same until 2.0, marked as deprecated so your editor strikes it through and says what to use; when a page gives both, the new name wins. Rename at your own pace; the tables say where each one is documented.

What changes in 2.0

A few things cannot keep an old name, because they are values a page compares, not names it calls. They stay as they are until 2.0:

  • A view moved by the keys says 'keys' (ViewSource), while a seek by the keys says 'keyboard' (SeekSource). In 2.0 both say 'keyboard'.
  • The hover event gives [ratio, time], while timeupdate gives [time, ratio]. In 2.0 both give the time first.
  • Every old name in the tables of 0.7's new names goes away.

The detail of the tags' events stays a list of the event's arguments, in the order on() gives them.

In 0.7: fixed

  • soundDescription() with a keys.backGrace that is not a number kept Shift+P from going back at all; it now takes the usual second.
  • silencesFromPeaks with a threshold over 0.5 left a silence open to the end of the sound.

Every change, and those of earlier versions, is in the changelog.
