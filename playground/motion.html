Motion

Some people get dizzy or distracted by things that move. In SonicFrame, decoration never moves unless you ask, and nothing moves for people who asked their system for less motion.

What moves, and when

Motion By default With reduced motion
The played part, while the sound plays on (it is the information) on
Bars growing in when peaks arrive (animate) on never
The reading button's pulse or fade (description.button.animation) off never
The zoom following the position (follow) off 'center' turns a page at a time
Live frequency bars off (a plugin) your call

Animation is a switch, its kind another option

Where SonicFrame offers motion, a boolean turns it on and a separate option picks its kind, with a default: 

createPlayer({
    container,
    src,
    a11y: {
        description: {
            button: { animation: true, animationType: 'fade' }, // 'pulse' by default
        },
    },
});

A kind it does not know falls back to the default, and the whole animation is skipped when the system asks for less motion.

Turning the grow-in off

new SonicFrame({ container, media, peaks, animate: false });
