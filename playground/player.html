Player basics

createPlayer puts a play button, the wave and the time together, and anything else you ask for: speed, skip buttons, volume, zoom, a minimap. It lives on its own entry, so an app that only draws waves never loads it.

One call

import { createPlayer } from 'sonicframe/player';

const player = createPlayer({
    container: document.querySelector('#player'),
    src: '/notes/42.ogg',
});

Given only src, the player makes its own <audio> and decodes the peaks in the browser the first time it comes near the screen. Give peaks (a list, a file loader, or { peaks, duration }) to skip the decoding, and preload: 'none' to load no audio until play.

Your own media element

Pass media instead of src to drive an element you already have, a video included. The player never stops or removes an element it did not make. 

createPlayer({ container, media: document.querySelector('video') });

The parts

A player is a line of parts. The default line is ['play', 'wave', 'time']; add any of these:

Part What it is
play The play button.
wave The wave (always there).
time, elapsed, remaining, duration The time, as the look's time says, or one of its forms.
speed Steps through the speeds (1×, 1.5×, 2×).
back, forward Skip a few seconds (the look's skip, 10).
volume The volume control; also volume: true.
zoomIn, zoomOut, zoomReset Zoom buttons; also zoom: { buttons: true }.
slot Your own element (an avatar, say), given back on destroy.
spacer Pushes the next parts to the end. 
createPlayer({
    container,
    src,
    look: { line: ['play', 'back', 'wave', 'forward', 'time', 'speed'] },
});

Parts and layouts covers the rest of the look: sizes, the line under the wave, the wave on top, and the voice-note arrangement on phones.

Changing it later

Method What it does
setTheme(name) Another theme; your own options still win.
setLook(look) Part of the look (rebuilds the player).
setPalette(palette) Other colors; nothing brings the theme's back.
setWave(wave) Another drawing.
setFont(font) Another font.
setVolume(show) Shows or hides the volume control.
on(event, listener) The wave's events, plus description.
destroy() Gives the container back; a media element the player made is stopped.

player.frame, player.button and player.volume reach the parts, and player.description gives the sound in words when it is on.
