Playing and seeking

People seek with a click, a drag or the keys; your code seeks, binds another sound and changes the drawing through a few methods. Playing itself stays with the media element.

How people seek

  • Click or tap: jumps there.
  • Drag (touch included): scrubs, and seeks once when released. A cancelled drag changes nothing.
  • Keys, with the wave focused: the arrows step by stepSeconds (5), Page Up and Page Down by largeStepRatio (a tenth), Home and End go to the ends.
  • A vertical swipe still scrolls the page (touch-action: pan-y).

Seeking from code

frame.seekTo(0.5); // the middle of the sound; the media moves too
frame.on('seek', ({ ratio, time, source }) => {
    console.log(`Moved to ${time} s by ${source}`);
});

Positions are ratios of the sound, from 0 to 1, so they never depend on the screen. source says who moved it: the pointer, the keyboard or the API.

Changing the sound

Method What it does
bind(media) Follow another audio or video element, or none (null).
setPeaks(source) New peaks: a list, { peaks, duration }, a loader or null.
setDuration(seconds) The length to use until the media knows its own.
setProgress(ratio) The position shown while no media is bound.

Changing the look

Method What it does
setWave(wave) Another drawing (a name) or some of its details.
setBar(bar) Part of the bar style (width, gap, radius…).
setColors(colors) Some of the colors.
setLabel(label) The accessible name.
refresh() Reads the colors again after your own theme switch (the system's dark mode is followed on its own).

Reading the state

progress (0 to 1), duration (seconds, or null), peaksDuration (the length the peaks came with) and view (the part in view) are read-only properties.
