Captions

A line of text under the wave: a transcript's first words, a title, a note. It fades out on the right, can carry badges, and its full text reaches screen readers.

Adding one

import { caption } from 'sonicframe';

frame.use(
    caption({
        text: 'I met a little elf-man, once, down where the lilies blow.',
        badges: ['auto', 'en'],
    }),
);

Hover it, focus the wave or tap the caption to see the tooltip with the fuller text.

For screen readers

The full text goes in the wave's aria-describedby, next to any ids your page put there (it never removes yours), so a screen reader reads it when the person reaches the wave. Text goes in as text, never as HTML.

Options

Option What it does
text The caption.
badges Up to three short marks before the text (['auto', 'pt']).
tooltip What the tooltip shows; left out, the caption again, longer.
tooltipLimit The most characters the tooltip shows, cut at a word.
size The line's height, in CSS pixels.

set(text) replaces the text (null removes the line and gives its room back to the wave), and setTooltip(content) the tooltip.
